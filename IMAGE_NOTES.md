# Food image notes

Sixteen `.webp` dish pictures in `img/dishes/` were AI-generated as illustrative food images for this course website. They are **not documentary photographs** of a particular prepared dish, and recipes and presentations vary across families and regions. The existing country photographs and map from the original site have been left unchanged.

The JSON file specifies each dish's image and preparation information. The explorer loads these fields through the existing `fetch()` and DOM-building logic.

## Culture page images
The four `img/culture-*.jpg` images are AI-generated illustrations added for the culture page. Their captions identify them as such; they are not documentary photographs.
