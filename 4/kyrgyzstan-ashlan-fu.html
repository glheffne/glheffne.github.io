Ashlan-Fu

Cuisine of Kyrgyzstan

About Ashlan-Fu

Ashlan-Fu is a cold and spicy noodle dish that is especially associated with the city of Karakol in Kyrgyzstan. The dish combines noodles with starch jelly and a flavorful mixture that commonly includes vinegar and chili.

Main Ingredients

  • Noodles
  • Starch jelly
  • Vinegar
  • Chili
  • Seasonings and herbs

Cultural Significance

Ashlan-Fu has roots in Dungan cuisine and has become closely associated with Karakol. It is a good example of how migration and cultural exchange have influenced the foods eaten in modern Kyrgyzstan.
