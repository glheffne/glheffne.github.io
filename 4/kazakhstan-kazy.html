Kazy

Traditional Cuisine of Kazakhstan

About Kazy

Kazy is a traditional sausage made from horse meat and fat. It is associated with the food traditions of Kazakhstan and other parts of Central Asia. The meat is seasoned and placed into a casing before being cooked.

Main Ingredients

  • Horse meat
  • Horse fat
  • Salt
  • Seasonings
  • Natural casing

Cultural Significance

Kazy reflects the historical importance of horses within Kazakh pastoral and nomadic traditions. It may be served during celebrations, gatherings, and special meals and is considered an important part of traditional Kazakh cuisine.
