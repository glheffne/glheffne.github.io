Manty

Traditional Cuisine of Turkmenistan

About Manty

Manty are dumplings found in Turkmen and other Central Asian cuisines. They are made by wrapping dough around a filling, which commonly contains meat and onions. The dumplings are then typically steamed until cooked.

Main Ingredients

  • Dough
  • Meat
  • Onions
  • Salt
  • Seasonings

Cultural Significance

Manty are part of a wider dumpling tradition found across Central Asia. Their presence in Turkmen cuisine demonstrates how neighboring cultures have influenced one another while developing their own variations of shared dishes.
