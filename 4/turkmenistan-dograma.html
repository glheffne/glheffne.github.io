Dograma

Traditional Cuisine of Turkmenistan

About Dograma

Dograma is a traditional Turkmen dish made by combining pieces of bread with cooked meat, onions, and broth. The bread and meat are broken into smaller pieces before the broth is added to create a hearty meal.

Main Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Meat
  • Onions
  • Broth
  • Seasonings

Cultural Significance

Dograma is closely connected with traditional Turkmen food culture. Its preparation can involve sharing and serving a large meal among family members or guests, reflecting the importance of food and hospitality in Turkmen gatherings.
