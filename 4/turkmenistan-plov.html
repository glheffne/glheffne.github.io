Plov

Traditional Cuisine of Turkmenistan

About Plov

Plov is a rice-based dish enjoyed throughout Central Asia, including Turkmenistan. It is commonly prepared by cooking rice with meat, vegetables, and seasonings. Different families and regions may prepare the dish in different ways.

Main Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Meat
  • Carrots
  • Onions
  • Oil
  • Seasonings

Cultural Significance

Plov is an example of a food tradition shared across Central Asia. In Turkmenistan, versions of the dish are served as part of meals and gatherings. Its presence in several Central Asian cuisines also demonstrates the cultural connections between countries in the region.
