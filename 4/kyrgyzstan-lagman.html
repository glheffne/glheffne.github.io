Lagman

Cuisine of Kyrgyzstan

About Lagman

Lagman is a popular noodle dish in Kyrgyzstan. It is commonly prepared using hand-pulled noodles served with meat, vegetables, and a flavorful sauce or broth. Different preparations can produce either a soup-like or fried version of the dish.

Main Ingredients

  • Hand-pulled noodles
  • Meat
  • Vegetables
  • Onions
  • Seasonings

Cultural Significance

Lagman demonstrates how Kyrgyzstan's cuisine has been influenced by neighboring cultures. The dish has strong connections to Dungan and Uyghur culinary traditions and has become a popular part of everyday food in Kyrgyzstan.
