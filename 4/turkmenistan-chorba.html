Chorba

Traditional Cuisine of Turkmenistan

About Chorba

Chorba is a type of soup found in Turkmen cuisine as well as other cuisines throughout Central Asia and surrounding regions. Versions of the soup can contain meat, vegetables, and a flavorful broth.

Main Ingredients

  • Meat
  • Broth
  • Onions
  • Vegetables
  • Herbs and seasonings

Cultural Significance

Chorba represents another example of the shared culinary traditions found throughout Central Asia and nearby regions. Different communities prepare their own versions using available meats, vegetables, herbs, and seasonings.
