Beshbarmak

Traditional Cuisine of Kyrgyzstan

About Beshbarmak

Beshbarmak is a traditional Kyrgyz dish made with boiled meat served with noodles and an onion-based broth. The meat may include lamb, beef, or horse meat. The name is commonly translated as "five fingers," referring to the tradition of eating the dish by hand.

Main Ingredients

  • Meat
  • Flat noodles
  • Onions
  • Broth
  • Seasonings

Cultural Significance

Beshbarmak is associated with Kyrgyz hospitality and is often prepared for important gatherings and honored guests. The dish also reflects the historical importance of livestock and nomadic traditions in Kyrgyz culture.
