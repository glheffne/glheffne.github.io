Plov

Traditional Cuisine of Uzbekistan

About Plov

Plov is one of the best-known dishes associated with Uzbek cuisine. It is a rice-based dish commonly prepared with meat, carrots, onions, oil, and seasonings. Different regions and families may prepare their own variations of the dish.

Main Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Meat
  • Carrots
  • Onions
  • Oil
  • Seasonings

Cultural Significance

Plov has an important place in Uzbek food culture and is commonly associated with hospitality and social gatherings. It may be prepared for family meals, celebrations, weddings, and other important occasions.
