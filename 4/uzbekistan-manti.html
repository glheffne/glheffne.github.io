Manti

Traditional Cuisine of Uzbekistan

About Manti

Manti are steamed dumplings commonly found in Uzbek and Central Asian cuisine. They are made by wrapping thin dough around a savory filling that often contains meat and onions. The dumplings are then steamed until the dough and filling are fully cooked.

Main Ingredients

  • Dough
  • Meat
  • Onions
  • Salt
  • Seasonings

Cultural Significance

Manti are part of a broader dumpling tradition shared across Central Asia. In Uzbekistan, they are prepared as a filling meal and demonstrate how similar foods can develop different traditions and variations across neighboring cultures.
