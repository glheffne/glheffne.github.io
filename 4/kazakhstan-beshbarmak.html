Beshbarmak

Traditional Cuisine of Kazakhstan

About Beshbarmak

Beshbarmak is a traditional Central Asian dish strongly associated with Kazakhstan. It is typically made with boiled meat served over flat noodles and is often accompanied by onions and broth.

Main Ingredients

  • Meat
  • Flat noodles
  • Onions
  • Broth

Cultural Significance

Beshbarmak is commonly connected with hospitality, family gatherings, and special occasions. The dish also reflects Kazakhstan's historical connections to pastoral and nomadic traditions.
