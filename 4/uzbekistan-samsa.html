Samsa

Traditional Cuisine of Uzbekistan

About Samsa

Samsa is a baked pastry commonly found in Uzbek cuisine. It consists of dough wrapped around a savory filling, which often contains meat and onions. Samsa can come in different shapes and may be baked in a traditional oven.

Main Ingredients

  • Dough
  • Meat
  • Onions
  • Fat or oil
  • Seasonings

Cultural Significance

Samsa is a popular food in Uzbekistan and can be found in markets, bakeries, restaurants, and homes. Its widespread availability makes it an example of traditional food that also remains part of everyday life.
