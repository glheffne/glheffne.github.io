Kuurdak

Traditional Cuisine of Kazakhstan

About Kuurdak

Kuurdak is a traditional Central Asian meat dish that is prepared by frying meat with onions and other ingredients. Different versions of the dish can be found throughout Central Asia, including Kazakhstan.

Main Ingredients

  • Meat
  • Onions
  • Cooking fat or oil
  • Salt
  • Seasonings

Cultural Significance

Kuurdak demonstrates the importance of meat-based dishes in traditional Central Asian cuisine. Variations of the dish are prepared across the region, making it another example of how neighboring Central Asian cultures share culinary traditions.
