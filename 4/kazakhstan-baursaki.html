Baursaki

Traditional Cuisine of Kazakhstan

About Baursaki

Baursaki are pieces of fried dough commonly found in Kazakh cuisine. They are usually prepared by frying pieces of dough until they become golden and fluffy. Baursaki can be served alongside other foods or eaten on their own.

Main Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Yeast
  • Milk or water
  • Salt
  • Oil for frying

Cultural Significance

Baursaki are associated with hospitality and are commonly served during family gatherings, celebrations, and other special occasions. They can be served with tea or alongside other traditional foods.
