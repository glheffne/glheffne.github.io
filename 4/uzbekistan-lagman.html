Lagman

Cuisine of Uzbekistan

About Lagman

Lagman is a noodle dish enjoyed in Uzbekistan and other parts of Central Asia. It commonly combines noodles with meat, vegetables, and a flavorful sauce or broth. Recipes and preparation styles can vary throughout the region.

Main Ingredients

  • Noodles
  • Meat
  • Vegetables
  • Onions
  • Seasonings

Cultural Significance

Lagman demonstrates the cultural exchange that has influenced Central Asian cuisine. Similar versions are eaten across several countries, while communities and families may develop their own methods of preparing the dish.
