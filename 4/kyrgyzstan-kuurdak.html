Kuurdak

Traditional Cuisine of Kyrgyzstan

About Kuurdak

Kuurdak is a traditional meat dish found in Kyrgyz cuisine. It is commonly prepared by frying meat with onions and potatoes. Different versions may use lamb, beef, horse meat, or organ meats.

Main Ingredients

  • Meat
  • Onions
  • Potatoes
  • Cooking fat or oil
  • Seasonings

Cultural Significance

Kuurdak has connections to the meat-based cooking traditions of Central Asia. Its simple and filling ingredients reflect the importance of livestock and hearty meals within traditional Kyrgyz food culture.
