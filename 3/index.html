Central Asian Culture & Cuisine

Project 2 Web Application Proposal

Topic Description

Central Asia is a region located between Europe, Russia, China, the Middle East, and South Asia. The region is commonly associated with Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Turkmenistan, and Uzbekistan, as well as other nearby countries. These countries have long histories influenced by nomadic societies, the Silk Road, neighboring cultures, and the movement of people and goods across the region. Food is an important part of understanding these cultures because traditional dishes often reflect the geography, available ingredients, history, and lifestyles of the people who live there.

I became interested in Central Asian culture because of my girlfriend's country of origin. Getting to learn more about her culture made me curious about the other countries in Central Asia and how their food, traditions, and history are similar and different. One problem with learning about Central Asian culture online is that information is often spread across many different websites and resources. Someone who is unfamiliar with the region may have difficulty understanding the similarities and differences between countries or discovering how food connects to their history and culture. My website addresses this problem by organizing information about Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Turkmenistan, and Uzbekistan into one easy-to-navigate, interactive website. Resources such as UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage website and Encyclopaedia Britannica provide useful cultural and historical information, but my website will focus on making these connections easier to explore in one place.

Web Application Description

The proposed website will be an interactive guide to the cultures and cuisines of Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Turkmenistan, and Uzbekistan. Users will be able to learn basic information about Central Asia and then use an interactive two-step selection process to explore traditional foods from each country.

Planned Pages

  1. Home Page: Introduces Central Asia and provides an overview of the four countries.
  2. Interactive Explore Page: Allows users to select a country and then select a traditional food from that country.
  3. Culture and History Page: Provides additional information about Central Asian geography, history, traditions, clothing, music, and cultural influences.
  4. Contact Us Page: Provides a form that will eventually allow users to submit their name, email address, topic, and message.

Final Information

Each of the 16 food choices will eventually display information using the same format. The final information will include the food's name, country of origin, description, main ingredients, cultural significance, images, and a map showing its geographic connection to Central Asia. Keeping the same information format for every choice will make the interactive experience consistent for users.

Additional Website Content

The Culture and History page will provide information that is separate from the interactive food selections. Planned topics include Central Asian geography, the Silk Road, nomadic traditions, traditional clothing, music and instruments, languages, celebrations, and the ways geography and history have influenced Central Asian cuisine.

Geography

An overview of Central Asia's geography and how the region's environment influenced its people and food.

The Silk Road

Information about the historical trade routes that connected Central Asia with surrounding regions.

Nomadic Traditions

An introduction to nomadic lifestyles and their influence on Central Asian culture and cuisine.

Culture

Additional information about clothing, music, languages, and cultural traditions.

Contact Us

The Contact Us page will eventually allow users to provide feedback about the website. The planned form will collect a user's name, email address, selected topic, and message. Users may also be able to submit suggestions for additional foods or cultural information. This information will not be collected or stored during the initial version of the project.
